# CTO review #14: metal, Montaj tezgâhı and the main flows (2026-11-28)

Scope: `origin/main` @ `4a68ffb` (B65, B66, MB1–MB4, T384, T385, T386–T389; D-130..D-133).
Previous review: `2026-11-23-cto-design-review-13.md`.

Method:

- Production build in a separate worktree, fresh data, port 3020, Playwright at 1440 × 900, light theme.
- Walked: new project → Parçalar (paste 5 rows, "Dolap ekle") → Dizilim (one click "En az fire", 2 sheets)
  → Simülasyon → Dışa aktar; Çizim (arc tool "a", three clicks, Ctrl+Z); ACM 4 mm with "Arka yüzden işle";
  aluminium 4 mm to Simülasyon and Dışa aktar; Montaj (mate Yan on Taban, "Oturuyor mu?", Patlat 100 %,
  İzometrik); Makineler → Yeni makine → Metal kesim; Ayarlar → Malzemeler.
- 9 screenshots in `2026-11-28-cto-14/`.

Not done: an ACM NC download (the seeded machine has no ACM set, so the D-130 header line and checklist
item were not seen), the Montaj föyü PDF page (not rendered), dark theme, 390 px, axe. Product code,
DECISIONS and the goldens were not touched.

## Summary

The wood path is calm and finished: one-click nest modes read well, Simülasyon and the export
checklist are still the strongest screens, and nothing broke on the way. The new parts do not yet
meet that bar. Metal is honest about what is blocked, but it tells the operator to do things that
cannot help (aluminium), and it does not carry its most dangerous fact, "this job is mirrored, lay the
sheet face down", to the two places the operator reads at the machine. The Montaj bench works
(mate, fit check, explode all did what they said) but its toolbar collides at 1440, the fit check calls
a loose part "oturuyor", and the paste toast sits on top of the drawing board's new arc options.

Field test 1 (Mach3, MDF 18) is **not affected** by any MUST below: all three are metal or editor issues.
They should land before anyone cuts ACM.

Architecture: the wizard ships 450.8 of its 460 kB budget (D-133). `tr.json` is one 199 kB file
(≈ 59 kB gzip, about 13 % of the budget) loaded on every route. T385 (split the messages per route)
should run before the next wizard feature, not after it busts the budget again. The bench in its own
lazy chunk is right; keep 3D-A..D (D-132) on the same rule from day one.

## MUST (3)

**M1. Back-face machining is not stated where the operator reads it.**
Screen: step 1 (ACM, `06`), Dışa aktar, NC file.
What is wrong: ACM defaults to "Arka yüzden işle" (traits `machinedFace: 'back'`); the program is then
mirrored. The fact is shown on the wizard badge, the PDF sheet page and the labels, but **not** in the
NC header and **not** in the export checklist. An operator who lays the sheet film side down the usual
way (or a reused file) gets mirrored parts and V-grooves on the visible face: wrong output, scrapped
panel.
Fix:
- [ ] NC header line when `machinedFace = back`: `(PLAKA ARKA YUZU USTTE: PARCALAR AYNALI)`, beside the D-130 material line
- [ ] a required export checklist item: "Plakayı arka yüzü üstte (film alta) yatırdım; parçalar aynalı kesilir."
- [ ] the independent checker asserts the header line when the project says back face (as D-128 does for vacuum)
- [ ] goldens unchanged (no golden uses back face); a new small fixture for ACM back face

**M2. The paste toast covers the arc options, and its "Geri al" sits on them.**
Screen: Parçalar → Çizim (`05`).
What is wrong: after a paste the toast "1 satır eklendi · Geri al" stays at the top of the canvas
(542–898 × 187–245 px) and fully covers the new arc wing "Sehim / 3 nokta / Merkez / Teğet"
(573–867 × 206–244). The toast's "Geri al" lies over the right end of the wing: a click meant for
"Teğet" undoes the paste. The same toast also covers the top of the Montaj canvas right after a paste.
Fix:
- [ ] workspace views (Çizim, Montaj, Dizilim) put toasts bottom-right, clear of every canvas toolbar
- [ ] switching the parts view (Liste / Çizim / Montaj) dismisses an open undo toast (the undo stays in Ctrl+Z)
- [ ] e2e: after a paste, `arc-options` is clickable at 1440 (no element on top, `elementFromPoint`)

**M3. Aluminium is a dead end that offers fixes which cannot work.**
Screen: step 1, Simülasyon (`07`), Dışa aktar, machine form (`12`).
What is wrong: D-130 blocks every aluminium NC until MB7, whatever the machine says. Step 1 still
shows "Kesim ayarı ekle" and "Makineyi düzenle: Metal kesim"; Simülasyon lists 3 errors for one cause;
the machine form says "Kapalıyken alüminyum işi dosya vermez", which reads as "open it and you get a
file". The user fills the machine, comes back, and is still blocked. The reason text also shows
internal task numbers: "(T358, T359)".
Fix:
- [ ] while `MATERIAL_NOT_RELEASED` holds, it is the only error shown for aluminium (the set and metal-machine errors are folded under it), and step 1 hides the two fix buttons
- [ ] text: "Alüminyum için G-code henüz verilmiyor: rampa giriş ve talaş sınırları ekleniyor. DXF, PDF ve etiketler kullanılabilir." (no task ids in `tr.json`)
- [ ] machine form hint adds: "Alüminyum G-code'u henüz kapalı; beyan şimdiden kaydedilir."

## POLISH (11)

**P1. ACM thickness hint says a set exists when it does not.** Step 1 (`06`). "Bu makinede kesim ayarı
olan kalınlıklar: 3, 4, 6 mm", then right below "4 mm için kesim ayarı yok". The chips merge the
profile's thicknesses with the kind presets (`mergeThicknesses`) under the profile label. Fix: mark
the chips that have a set (✓) and label the row "Bu malzemenin kalınlıkları".

**P2. ACM keeps the wood sheet.** Step 1 (`06`). Switching MDF → ACM leaves the sheet at 2.800 × 2.070
(no ACM preset pressed, preview shows a wood sheet), and the grain row says "Bu malzemede damar yok ·
Damar yönü ver". Fix: on a kind change pick the kind's first preset when the current size is not one of
its presets; for ACM/aluminium say "Film / fırça yönü" instead of "damar".

**P3. One click for ACM starting values.** Step 1. D-130 allows ACM NC with researched values, but the
seeded machine has no ACM set, so every new ACM project starts with an error. Fix: the error offers
"Başlangıç değerleriyle ekle" (T328 table, marked unverified) next to "Kesim ayarı ekle".

**P4. "Oturuyor mu?" is half under the view toolbar.** Montaj (`10`, `11`). At 1440 the button spans
x 913–1048 and the zoom/fit cluster starts at ≈ 997. Fix: one toolbar row that wraps, or collapse the
tool labels to icons + tooltip below 1600 px.

**P5. The fit check calls a loose part fine.** Montaj (`11`). "3 parça · 1 temas · Her şey oturuyor"
while "Yan · 2. adet" touches nothing. Fix: a "Değmeyen parça: Yan · 2. adet" line (info, not error);
"Her şey oturuyor" only when every shown part touches another.

**P6. Mate mode stays on while exploded.** Montaj (`11`). Patlat 100 % says parts cannot move, while the
hint still asks "Sabit kalacak parçada bir yüzey tıklayın". Fix: exploding leaves Taşı/Döndür/Oturt
disabled with the same reason as a tooltip, and returns to the previous mode at 0 %.

**P7. The fit panel pushes the part's position below the fold.** Montaj (`11`). Fix: open the results as
a panel over the canvas's lower-left corner (like the hint), or a tab in the right column.

**P8. Kenar payı helper disagrees with D-129.** Dizilim (`02`). "Parça arası pay" shows the effective
value ("Ø8 + pay 11,1 = 19,1 mm"), but "Kenar payı" says "Takım için en az 8 mm" while tabs make it 11.
Fix: same pattern: "Köprüyle en az Ø8 + 3 = 11 mm".

**P9. "Tüm program" chips look like code.** Simülasyon / Dışa aktar error lists (`07`). Monospace grey
chip in front of plain Turkish. Fix: drop the chip when the scope is the whole program; keep it only
for a sheet or part ("Plaka 2", "Yan · 1").

**P10. Research hedges leak into the UI.** Machine form (`12`): "Paso tavanını ölçekler; doğrulanacak."
(3 strings in `tr.json` end in "doğrulanacak"). Fix: say what the user should do: "Başlangıç değeri;
deneme kesimiyle ayarlayın."

**P11. Malzemeler list has no column names.** Ayarlar → Malzemeler (`13`). "Yok", "0°" and the size
pairs stand without headers; metal kinds do not appear until added. Fix: a header row (Kalınlık, Plaka
ölçüleri, Damar / yön, Kesim ayarı) and, when no metal entry exists, a one-line hint "ACM ve alüminyum
için Malzeme ekle".

## Good (keep)

- One-click nest modes: each card states its result ("2 plaka · fire %53,8"); no extra "Diz" button.
- Aluminium is blocked consistently on Simülasyon and Dışa aktar, and DXF/PDF/labels stay available.
- The bench says "Yalnızca görünüm: dizilim, takım yolu ve G-kodu değişmez" and the fit check prints its
  own tolerances: the right tone for a workshop.
- Face-to-face mate landed exactly (Y = 27 for 18 mm on 18 mm) and survives a reload.
- The export checklist and "Simülasyon air cut'ın yerine geçmez" banner are unchanged and still clear.
